# Escalation
Escalate when the issue is security-sensitive, requires account-specific action, or cannot be answered reliably from the knowledge base.
Every escalation should include a short justification.

## Example justifications
- "Escalated: suspected account compromise, security-sensitive."
- "Escalated: needs an account-specific billing action not covered by the knowledge base."
- "Escalated: documented troubleshooting did not resolve the issue."
