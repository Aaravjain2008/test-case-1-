# Refunds
Refund eligibility depends on the product's refund policy.
Do not promise a refund unless the applicable policy confirms that the user qualifies.

## Examples
- A user asks for a refund after a failed payment: check the refund policy first, then explain the outcome. Do not promise a refund before confirming eligibility.
- A user is charged twice: collect the invoice details and escalate if the policy does not cover the case.
