# Billing
Billing questions include invoices, charges, refunds, and payment failures.
Account-specific billing disputes should be escalated when the knowledge base does not provide enough information.

## Examples
- The user asks for a copy of an invoice: explain where invoices are listed in the account, and do not guess amounts.
- A payment failed: ask for the error shown and the date, and point to the documented payment troubleshooting steps.
- The user disputes a specific charge and the knowledge base has no matching information: escalate with a short justification.
